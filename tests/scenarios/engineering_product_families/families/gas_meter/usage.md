# GAS_METER middle-port numeric Family

This Family retains the selected product hierarchy and geometry binding roles: root `body` is one `IfcFlowMeter` with fixed 96-solid mapped Geometry; `left_fitting` and `right_fitting` are `IfcPipeFitting` children, each using the selected six-detail connector function shape. It retains the middle-of-body datum and opposed full-frame internal joins. Supply `external_constraints` for `gas_left` and `gas_right`, plus product origin O. Each condition is `finite_segment` with `start_m`, `end_m`, positive `outer_diameter_m` and `direction_relation: "same"`. Each segment starts toward the product and ends toward the continuing pipe; its outward unit direction is start→end. Placement uses the entire finite interval. The solved left/right ODs feed the connector Geometry parameter mappings; no public installation A/B/OD parameters remain.

The standard model builds the original nominal design targets internally and
runs the same numeric solver, with empty public parameters and no external
constraints. Backend translates that local model once at product origin O.

Let the shared horizontal right axis be `u`, its station reference be the left source A, and the body datum station be `x`. The body has local datum `(0,0,.14)` m and internal attachments `(±.2,0,.14)` m. The Program solves `x`, physical connector lengths `ℓL/ℓR`, and exterior end stations `x−.2−ℓL` / `x+.2+ℓR` inside the two finite A/B windows. Connector minimum length is `2c+.0104` m, where the last 1.2 mm is visible terminal margin; 0.1 m is a nominal objective only. It minimizes `(ℓL−.1)^2+(ℓR−.1)^2` on the finite linear feasible region, then selects its smallest possible `x`. M2's separated windows have a .3/.3 m valid witness; the old .15 m length cap does not apply.

For self realization, root placement occurs once at O. The body fragment rotates only in yaw and translates to `C−O−(0,0,.14)`, mapping its local **middle** datum to solved C. The left and right connector fragment origins are `C−.2u` and `C+.2u`; their own `+X` points outward as `−u` and `+u`. Both profile-less joint frames coincide with the body's original z=.14 m attachments and oppose their normals with the same +Z reference. The only two external GAS interfaces come from the real nipple end faces and their source OD.

The standard construction uses source design coordinates local to the product root. Its nominal source line is at z=0, matching the selected historical standard model: the body's local z=.14 datum is lowered to that line, while its two attachments remain in the housing middle. A nonzero standard origin translates the same local model once. A self geometry scenario can supply lines at z=0 or elsewhere without changing the body's local z=.14 annotations. Noncollinear or tilted axes, including M3's 30°, return `INFEASIBLE` before Geometry. A physically impossible finite window returns `INFEASIBLE`; a truly zero-length source segment returns `INFEASIBLE`, while a nonzero direction that underflows arithmetic returns `AMBIGUOUS`. A positive finite source window is not rejected by an artificial minimum length.
